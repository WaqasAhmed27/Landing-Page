Zero-Code POS Digital Receipt Integration Explained

How TapTile connects seamlessly with your existing counter setup — delivering instant digital receipts in real time with absolutely zero billing software modifications.

How Zero-Code POS Integration Works

Zero-code POS integration allows retail stores to deploy digital receipt functionality without changing a single line of code in their billing software. A compact TapTile counter device works alongside your existing POS and thermal printer setup, capturing sales data at the counter and generating an instant digital receipt.

Because the integration operates seamlessly alongside your counter equipment, your checkout staff never have to learn a new workflow. Cashiers ring up sales exactly as they always have, and paper receipts continue printing uninterrupted whenever required. TapTile simply provides an instant, paperless handoff directly to your customer.

Why Retailers Love TapTile Integration

Traditional digital receipt solutions require invasive software overhauls, expensive POS upgrades, or manual cashier data entry (asking for customer phone numbers or email addresses). TapTile eliminates all three obstacles completely.

The counter device generates a structured digital copy and makes it available to the customer in under two seconds. Your existing printer output remains completely unaffected, guaranteeing zero downtime and zero risk to checkout speed.
