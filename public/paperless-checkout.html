The Hidden Costs of Thermal Paper

When calculating receipt costs, most retailers only look at the bulk price of paper rolls. However, the true Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for paper receipts includes:

  • Thermal Roll Procurement: 80x80mm rolls typically yield 250-300 receipts.
  • Printer Maintenance: Thermal print heads have a rated lifespan (e.g., 150km of paper). Heavy printing requires frequent replacements (PKR 8,000 - 15,000 per head).
  • FBR Compliance Waste: Printing FBR QR codes adds 2-3 inches to every receipt, increasing paper consumption by up to 25%.
